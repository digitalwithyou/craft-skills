# Writing in English

Adapted from the approved Dutch copy (see [translation.md](translation.md)). British or American spelling per the voice guide; default British for Belgian organizations. Apply [web-writing.md](web-writing.md) first.

## Conventions

- Sentence case headings by default ("How we work in four steps").
- Numbers: 1,250.50; 15%; €25. Dates: 10 November 2026 (British) or November 10, 2026 (American). Times: 9:30 am, 9 am to 5 pm.
- Phone numbers in international format: +32 15 12 34 56.
- Belgian place names: the English exonym where one exists (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, Bruges, Mechelen stays Mechelen).

## AI tells to remove

Based on the patterns catalogued in Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" guide (and the humanizer skill built on it):

- **Significance inflation**: "stands as a testament to", "plays a pivotal/crucial role", "marks a significant milestone", "underscores the importance".
- **Promotional filler**: nestled, vibrant, rich tapestry, boasts, breathtaking, world-class, state-of-the-art, cutting-edge, best-in-class, renowned (unless proven).
- **AI vocabulary**: delve, leverage, seamless, robust, elevate, unlock, empower, navigate (the landscape), realm, embark, foster, streamline, game-changer, holistic, synergy.
- **Negative parallelism**: "It's not just X, it's Y", "not only… but also".
- **Rule of three** everywhere, especially triplets of adjectives.
- **Superficial -ing tails**: "…, highlighting the importance of quality", "…, ensuring a smooth experience".
- **Openers and closers**: "In today's fast-paced world", "Whether you're X or Y", "Ready to…?", "In summary", "Ultimately".
- **Vague attribution**: "experts say", "studies show", "many customers" without a source.
- **Formatting tells**: em dashes for every aside, Title Case Headings, bold scattered in paragraphs, emoji bullets.
- **Hedge stacks**: "may potentially", "can help to possibly".

## Checklist

```
- [ ] Reads like a native writer wrote it, not like Dutch in English words
- [ ] No pattern from the list above
- [ ] Spelling variant consistent (British/American)
- [ ] Facts identical to the Dutch source; no new claims
- [ ] Headings sentence case; links and buttons say what happens
- [ ] SEO title/description/slug written in English for English searches
- [ ] Alt texts translated
```
