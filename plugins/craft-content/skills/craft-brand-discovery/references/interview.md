# Discovery interview

Ask in rounds of 3–5 questions. Skip what research already answered and show the client what you found so they can correct it. Ask in Dutch unless the client prefers another language.

## Contents

- Round 1: purpose and success
- Round 2: audiences and their tasks
- Round 3: positioning and proof
- Round 4: voice
- Round 5: visual identity
- Round 6: practicalities

## Round 1: purpose and success

- What must the website achieve for the organization in the next year? Pick the one most important outcome (leads, applications, bookings, sales, reputation, fewer phone calls).
- What should a visitor do before leaving? What counts as a successful visit?
- What do people currently call or email about that the site should answer?

## Round 2: audiences and their tasks

- Who are the two or three most important visitor groups? For each: what do they want to get done, what do they already know, what makes them hesitate?
- Are there audiences that matter in other languages (EN, FR)? Is the content the same for them, or different?
- Who decides and who influences (B2B: buyer, user, technical reviewer, procurement)?

## Round 3: positioning and proof

- In one sentence: what do you do, for whom, and what is different about how you do it?
- What do customers say when they recommend you? (Ask for real phrases, reviews, emails.)
- What can you prove: numbers, certifications, named clients (with permission), cases, years, awards, guarantees?
- What do competitors all claim that you don't want to sound like?
- What do you *not* do, or who is not a good fit?

## Round 4: voice

- If the organization were a person at a reception, how would they talk? Three words. Then: which words would they never use?
- `u` or `je`? Does it differ per audience or channel?
- Show 2–3 example sites or texts you like (any sector) and one you dislike. What exactly appeals or irritates?
- How formal should EN and FR be (FR: `vous` by default)? British or American English?
- Humor: never, light, or part of the identity?

Then run the calibration step from SKILL.md.

## Round 5: visual identity

- Logo files and variations (color, black, white, icon only); any rules for use?
- Brand colors (hex if known), fonts (license and web font availability), existing templates.
- Photography: own photos available? Style (people at work, products, locations; posed vs documentary)? Anything to avoid?
- Illustration and icons: existing set? Line or filled, rounded or sharp, monochrome or colored?
- Figma or Sketch files we can use? Who is the designer, if any?

## Round 6: practicalities

- Who writes and approves content? Who publishes in the CMS?
- Legal or sector rules for claims (health, finance, food and alcohol, environmental claims)?
- Terms with fixed translations (product names, legal names, job titles)?
- Deadlines, campaigns or seasonality to plan around?
