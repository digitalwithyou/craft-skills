# craft-content

Content skills for Craft CMS 5 websites, optimised for DWY client projects. For editors, clients and developers, in Claude (claude.ai, Claude Desktop, Claude Code) or ChatGPT (ChatGPT, Codex).

- **craft-brand-discovery**: researches the organization, interviews the client, calibrates the voice with real samples, and writes a voice guide, visual guide, content strategy, NL/EN/FR glossary and a short AI brief for the site's MCP server.
- **craft-preparing-pages**: plans a page or section, maps it to the site's existing blocks, writes Dutch (Belgium) copy without AI clichés, adapts it to English and French, plans visuals, and creates **drafts only** on staging or production through Craft's MCP server. Publishing stays with people.

Install in Claude with `craft-content@dwy-craft` from the `digitalwithyou/craft-skills` marketplace, or upload the skill zips in Claude or ChatGPT.

Setup: connect the site's Craft MCP server (Craft 5.12+, `https://<site>/admin/mcp`) per environment, as a custom connector in Claude or a Developer-mode app in ChatGPT, and sign in with a Craft account that can create drafts but not publish. Creating drafts from ChatGPT needs a Business, Enterprise or Edu workspace. Your developer sets this up per site.
